A Google Chrome extension written in JavaScript. Pressing Ctrl+Shift while hovering over a word shows the Wiktionary entry for that word in an inline window.

When I moved from Firefox to Chrome, I missed the *Wiktionary and Google Translate* add-on I had been using, so I made this extension and published it to the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ogjdcbnhgjgabidifpnpiidgbkhlpnof).
