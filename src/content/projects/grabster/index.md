A summer job as a backend and navigation programmer on Grabster's Android app, made with [React Native](https://reactnative.dev/).

Grabster was an app that made it easy to buy and sell homemade food. Whenever you were hungry, you could pick up your phone and see what was cooking in your neighborhood.
