*Settle your disputes through Holmgang. But pay heed to the rituals, or die like a Niding!*

Holmgang is a local 1v1 2D fighting game made for Global Game Jam 2016. Rituals act as gameplay modifiers throughout the fight. I was the primary gameplay programmer on a team of four, and the game was made with [GameMaker](https://gamemaker.io/).
